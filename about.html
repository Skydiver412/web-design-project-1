A Little About My Skydiving Life

Skydiving

Skydiving has been a big part of my life. Over the years, I have made more than 1,000 skydives. I don't jump nearly as often as I used to, but I still try to make a few jumps each year.

These days, I enjoy being around the sport and making the occasional jump at Skydive City in Zephyrhills, Florida.
